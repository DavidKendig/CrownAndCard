The Library opens from the north side of the Private Party room through a two-metre passage. Six wall bookcases and four freestanding bookcase sections flank clear aisles, with two reading tables, upholstered chairs, open books and warm chandeliers.

[Bookshelf front PNG](../../res/materials/library-bookshelf.png) was generated with the built-in image_gen tool on 2026-09-29. [Exact prompt](prompts.json).

The full-bleed image shows four shelves of antique books inside a mahogany frame. LibraryArt samples it at 192x256, converts it to the master palette and maps it once over each shelf face, with front and rear panels on freestanding cases. All panels share one texture and mesh. Solid map boxes provide shelf depth, wooden sides, tops and collision.
